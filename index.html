Do I have to sell my property?

No. The first step is understanding the situation and the options that may be available.

Can you help before probate is opened?

Yes. We can discuss the property circumstances, likely ownership questions and practical next steps before or during probate.

What if several heirs or family members are involved?

Multiple-owner situations are common. We can help organize the property information and identify what may need to happen next.

Do you provide legal or financial advice?

No. Florida Property Assistance is not a law firm, government agency or financial adviser. We provide property-related information and may suggest consulting an appropriately licensed professional.
