A calm path forward

Property problems are rarely just about the property.

A death in the family, missed taxes, an old deed, multiple heirs or an approaching sale date can quickly become overwhelming. Our role is to understand the full situation, explain realistic paths forward and help you take the next step with confidence.

Sometimes that means coordinating with title professionals or attorneys. Sometimes it means resolving the property through a direct sale with a separate purchasing entity. The right answer depends on your circumstances—not on a one-size-fits-all pitch.

Frequently asked questions

Start with the facts.

These answers are general information, not legal or financial advice.

Do I have to sell my property?

No. The first conversation is about understanding your circumstances and possible options. There is no obligation to sell.

Is Florida Property Assistance a law firm?

No. We are not a law firm, title company, financial advisor or government agency. Legal matters should be reviewed by a qualified Florida attorney.

Can you help when several heirs are involved?

We can help gather information and discuss possible next steps. Probate, heirship and disputed ownership issues may require an attorney or title professional.

Does Florida Property Assistance buy property?

No. Florida Property Assistance is the assistance and intake brand. Any potential purchase would be handled separately and disclosed under the actual purchasing entity.

How quickly will someone contact me?

We aim to respond promptly during normal business hours. Time-sensitive foreclosure or tax-sale matters should be identified in the form or by phone.
